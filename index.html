MADE OF DATA. FELT AS A MOMENT.

A different way
to feel the numbers.

This sea is drawn, not filmed. Wave height shapes its scale and palette. Wave period sets its pace. Direction guides its sideways movement and the compass above.

With sound enabled, a synthesized ocean follows the same data: higher waves create a fuller sound, and longer periods create slower swells. These are artistic interpretations, not recordings from the coast.

Conditions come from Open-Meteo marine models, not a live camera or a local sensor. Refreshing retrieves the latest available values; they may stay the same between model updates. This is an artwork, not a navigation tool.
